# Accessibility Statement

MediMate aims to make medicine management and health information easier to access for patients and their caregivers.

## Accessibility Goals

- Provide clear labels and understandable instructions.
- Aim for readable text and straightforward navigation.
- Support keyboard-friendly interactions and responsive layouts.
- Make medicine schedules and notifications easy to understand.

## Supported Environments

MediMate is a browser-based application intended for use on modern browsers. Notification availability depends on browser support, permissions, and device settings.

## Known Limitations

Accessibility has not yet been independently audited. Some pages or interactive elements may have usability or accessibility limitations.

## Reporting an Accessibility Issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please open an issue in the GitHub repository describing the affected page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or device involved.

Please do not include private health information.
