# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We aim to make participation in the MediMate project respectful, welcoming, and constructive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Accept differing viewpoints.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Respect privacy and never share another person's sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory conduct.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions.

## Reporting Concerns

Report concerns to the repository owner privately. Include enough information to explain the concern, but avoid sharing sensitive personal or health information.

The repository owner will review reports and determine appropriate next steps.
